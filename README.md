# Static Structural Analysis of Conventional Sugar Mill Roller Shaft

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the **3D modelling and static structural analysis of a conventional sugar mill roller shaft** using ANSYS. The objective is to evaluate the structural performance of the roller shaft under specified loading conditions and compare the suitability of two engineering materials, **45C8 steel and EN47 steel**, for the application.

The roller shaft is an important mechanical component in a sugar mill, where it experiences significant mechanical loads during the crushing process. Structural analysis helps identify critical stress regions, evaluate deformation, and assess the factor of safety to support material selection and reliable component design.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a three-dimensional roller shaft model using ANSYS DesignModeler.
- Perform static structural analysis using ANSYS Mechanical.
- Evaluate the stress distribution and total deformation under specified loading conditions.
- Determine the factor of safety for the selected materials.
- Compare 45C8 steel and EN47 steel based on their structural performance under identical loading and boundary conditions.
- Assess the suitability of each material for the sugar mill roller shaft application.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **ANSYS DesignModeler:** 3D geometric modelling of the roller shaft.
- **ANSYS Mechanical:** Static structural simulation and post-processing.
- **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** Numerical evaluation of structural behaviour.
- **Meshing:** Discretization of the 3D geometry into finite elements.
- **Engineering Materials:** Comparative evaluation of 45C8 steel and EN47 steel.

## ⚙️ Methodology

### 1. 3D Geometric Modelling

A three-dimensional model of the conventional sugar mill roller shaft was developed in ANSYS DesignModeler. The geometry was prepared to represent the component for subsequent finite element analysis.

### 2. Material Definition

Two candidate materials, 45C8 steel and EN47 steel, were selected for comparative analysis. Their relevant mechanical properties were assigned in ANSYS according to the material data used in the study.

### 3. Meshing

The 3D model was discretized into a finite element mesh. Meshing enabled the numerical evaluation of stress and deformation throughout the roller shaft. The mesh quality and element distribution are important considerations for obtaining reliable simulation results.

### 4. Boundary Conditions and Loading

Appropriate supports, constraints, and loading conditions were applied to represent the specified operating conditions of the roller shaft. The same loading and boundary conditions were used for both materials to maintain a consistent basis for comparison.

### 5. Static Structural Analysis

Static structural simulations were performed in ANSYS Mechanical to investigate the shaft's response under the applied loads. The analysis focused on the following parameters:

- **Equivalent (von Mises) Stress:** Evaluates the stress distribution and identifies regions of potentially high stress.
- **Total Deformation:** Measures the resultant displacement of the roller shaft under the applied loading.
- **Factor of Safety:** Assesses the margin between the material's specified strength and the calculated stress, based on the adopted failure criterion.

### 6. Material Comparison

The simulation results for 45C8 steel and EN47 steel were compared to examine differences in stress distribution, deformation, and factor of safety. The comparison supports an engineering assessment of the two materials for the intended application, subject to the assumptions and limitations of the analysis.

## ⚠️ Scope and Limitations

This project focuses on static structural analysis under the specified loading and boundary conditions. The accuracy of the results depends on the geometric model, material properties, mesh quality, loading assumptions, and support conditions.

Dynamic loading, fatigue life, contact behaviour, and experimental validation are outside the scope unless separately investigated.
